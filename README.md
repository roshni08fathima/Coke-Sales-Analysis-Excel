# Coke Sales Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Coca-Cola sales data across U.S. retailers and regions using Microsoft Excel 365. The objective is to identify sales trends, compare retailer and regional performance, examine pricing and sales volume, and evaluate profitability.

## Business Objective

How can Coca-Cola optimize sales and profitability across U.S. retailers?

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains transactional and operational information, including:

- Retailer and Retailer ID
- Invoice Date
- Region, State, and City
- Beverage Brand
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Total Sales
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel 365
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Bar charts and line charts
- Conditional Formatting for regional sales heatmaps
- Slicers for interactive filtering
- Currency, percentage, and date formatting

## Analysis Performed

1. **Retailer Performance:** Compared total sales across retailers.
2. **Regional Performance:** Analyzed sales across U.S. regions.
3. **Monthly Sales Trends:** Examined monthly revenue patterns and seasonal variation.
4. **Profitability Analysis:** Compared operating profit and average operating margin.
5. **Pricing and Sales Volume:** Explored the relationship between price per unit, units sold, and total sales.
6. **Monthly Operating Profit:** Examined how operating profit changed over time.
7. **Interactive Dashboard:** Combined visualizations and a Region slicer for exploration.

## Key Findings

The initial analysis identified Sodapop as the highest-sales retailer among those displayed. The West region generated the highest total sales in the regional comparison.

The monthly sales analysis helps identify peak and slower sales periods, while the operating profit and margin analyses provide additional insight into profitability.

The price and sales volume comparison explores associations between pricing, quantity sold, and revenue.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate successful retailer strategies and opportunities to improve performance at lower-sales retailers.
- Use monthly sales patterns to support inventory and promotional planning.
- Monitor operating profit and operating margin alongside total revenue.
- Evaluate pricing decisions alongside sales volume and profitability.
- Use regional performance comparisons to guide further investigation and resource allocation.

## Deliverables

- Excel workbook containing the sales analysis, PivotTables, charts, heatmap, and interactive dashboard.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can transform sales data into visual insights that support retail performance evaluation, demand planning, and profitability analysis.
